I am a Lecturer in Health Economics in the Department of Economics, City St George's, University of London. Prior to that, I was a Health Economics Researcher in the Health Economics Research Centre (HERC), Nuffield Department of Population Health at the University of Oxford. My research focuses on applying econometric techniques to questions in health economics.

I graduated with a BSc in Economics from the University of Southampton in 2016. I then studied for an MSc and a PhD in Economics, also at the University of Southampton, both funded through an ESRC DTC 1+3 Research Studentship. Prior to joining HERC, I was an Early Career Teaching and Research Fellow in Economics at St Edmund Hall, University of Oxford, where I am now a Fellow by Special Election. At St Edmund Hall I primarily taught microeconomics-related subjects to undergraduates.
